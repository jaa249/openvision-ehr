OpenVision - before you install

OpenVision stores patient health information on this computer. Your practice is responsible for keeping it safe.

- Follow the patient data privacy and health record laws that apply where you practise (for example HIPAA in the United States, GDPR in the European Union, PIPEDA in Canada, or your country's equivalent), including how long records must be kept.
- Turn on full-disk encryption on this computer (BitLocker on Windows). OpenVision's database is not encrypted by OpenVision itself.
- Give every person their own Windows account and their own OpenVision account. Never share sign-ins. Lock the screen when you step away.
- Make regular backups, keep them encrypted and away from this computer, and test restoring them.
- Keep Windows updated and protected against malware.
